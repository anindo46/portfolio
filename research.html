Spatiotemporal shoreline dynamics and multi-model prediction using DSAS and machine learning along the Bhola island coast, Bangladesh

Anindo Paul Sourav, Mahfuj Ahmed, Md Manjurul Islam, Mahbubul Alam, Ummya Ayman Satil, Arabe Khan, Irteja Hasan, Nafisa Nawal Mimmi, and Dhiman Kumer Roy.

Study overview

This peer-reviewed study investigates long-term shoreline dynamics along the Bhola Island coast of Bangladesh. It identifies patterns of erosion and accretion, then forecasts likely shoreline positions for 2026, 2030, 2040, and 2050 to support evidence-based coastal planning.

The analysis integrates Landsat satellite imagery, MNDWI-based shoreline extraction, the Digital Shoreline Analysis System (DSAS), and multiple machine-learning models. Together, these approaches document historical shoreline movement and evaluate future change across a highly dynamic coastal environment.

Approach

Satellite archive: Landsat imagery for consistent long-term shoreline observation.
Shoreline mapping: MNDWI-based extraction and DSAS-derived change metrics.
Spatial coverage: 2,244 transects across seven shoreline segments.
Forecasting: Multiple machine-learning models assessed for future shoreline prediction.
